Understanding the Measurements

Community monitoring sensors record several indicators of air quality and environmental conditions. These measurements help identify patterns, but they do not identify specific pollution sources on their own.

TVOC and TVOC Index

Total volatile organic compounds (TVOC) provide a broad indicator of gases detected by the sensor. VOCs can contribute to ground-level ozone production, respiratory issues, and can accumulate in water and soils, disrupting terrestrial and aquatic life. The TVOC Index describes relative changes in gas conditions. Neither measurement identifies individual chemicals. TVOC readings should not be treated as a regulatory health threshold.

References: Jiang et al.(2025). Environmental and Health Risk Assessments of Volatile Organic Compounds (VOCs) Based on Source Apportionment-A Case Study in Harbin, a Megacity in Northeastern China. Toxics. 2025 Dec 31;14(1):46.

PM1, PM2.5, and PM10

Particulate matter (PM) refers to tiny particles suspended in the air. PM1, PM2.5, and PM10 describe particles within different size ranges, measured in micrograms per cubic meter (µg/m³). These measurements were collected less frequently than some other variables.

References: US Environmental Protection Agency. (2025) , Crinnnion W.(2017). Particulate Matter Is a Surprisingly Common Contributor to Disease. Integr Med (Encinitas). 16(4):8-12.

CO₂

Carbon dioxide (CO₂) is measured in parts per million (ppm). Changes can reflect ventilation, nearby people, and environmental conditions. CO₂ is not a direct measure of outdoor industrial pollution.

References: US Environmental Protection Agency. (2026) , Bardi et al. (2025). Carbon dioxide as a pollutant: the risks on human health and the stability of the biosphere, Environmental Science Advances.4(9). 1364-1372.

Air Quality Score (AQS) and NOx Index

These are sensor-generated indicators rather than direct measurements of individual pollutants. AQS is not the same as the official U.S. Air Quality Index (AQI). The NOx Index does not directly report nitrogen oxide concentrations in ppm or ppb.

References: Atmotube (2025)

Temperature, Humidity, and Pressure

These variables describe local environmental conditions. They can influence sensor readings and help provide context for changes in air quality.

These are community monitoring measurements, not regulatory-grade assessments. Missing observations should not be interpreted as clean air or zero pollution.
